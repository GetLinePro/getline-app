GetLine VPN is the official Android client for the GetLine service.

Connect with your GetLine subscription, choose a server, and manage VPN on a simple home screen. Advanced settings remain available for power users.

<h3>Highlights</h3>
<ul>
<li>Import a GetLine subscription URL (or QR); open the web cabinet for account management</li>
<li>One-tap connect and disconnect</li>
<li>Subscription refresh and server selection</li>
<li>Local VPN using the Mihomo core</li>
<li>Open-source GPLv3 fork of Clash Meta for Android (CMFA)</li>
</ul>
